## Halo 0.2.0: smoother, plus five new features

**Smooth at any refresh rate**
- Animation now runs at your monitor's own refresh rate (60, 144 or 240 Hz) and draws nothing while Halo sits idle.
- Hovers fade, the selected page and options slide, the battery number counts up, and pages glide in.
- Text layouts are cached, so each frame takes far less work.
- Turn on **Settings → Advanced → Frame rate counter** to see the rate.
- **Animations: Reduced** turns motion off. Halo also follows Windows' "Show animations" setting.

**New**
- **Automatic profiles per game:** attach apps to a profile ("Add a game or app" on each profile). While one of them is in front, Halo switches to that profile, and switches back to your settings when you leave it.
- **Hotkeys:** Ctrl+Alt+Up/Down steps through your DPI presets, and Ctrl+Alt+P goes to the next profile, from anywhere.
- **On-screen display:** a small popup at the bottom of the screen when DPI, polling rate or the profile changes from a hotkey, the tray or an automatic switch.
- **Battery history:** a 7-day graph on Overview, with how long the battery should last at your usual pace and when you last charged. The tray tooltip shows the time left too.
- **Polling test** (Performance): move the mouse and see the report rate Windows actually receives, with a peak and a live graph.
